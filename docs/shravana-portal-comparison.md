## SHRAVANA PORTAL: Comparison and Functionality

SHRAVANA PORTAL is designed to make grievance resolution measurable, transparent, and citizen-led. Compared with the current government CPGRAMS portal, which primarily records complaints, routes them to departments, and communicates official responses, SHRAVANA adds an accountability layer around the resolution journey. It tracks assignment, review, response, service-level deadlines, evidence, escalation, and the citizen’s confirmation of the outcome. A department response alone cannot silently close a case: the citizen can confirm resolution, reopen an inadequate response, or trigger escalation.

The portal begins with a guided intake form. It classifies a complaint, identifies the responsible department, checks whether required details such as location and date are missing, and directs out-of-scope requests—such as RTI applications—to the appropriate official channel. Valid grievances receive a unique reference, status timeline, SLA due date, and next steps. Citizens can upload supporting evidence, review departmental updates, and record whether the service was delivered. Reopened cases return to review, while pending or breached cases can move through defined escalation stages.

For officials and administrators, dashboards summarize citizen-confirmed resolution, average resolution time, SLA breach rate, and reopen rate. These outcome-focused measures reveal whether departments solved problems rather than merely issued replies. Audit events and evidence metadata improve traceability, while deterministic routing keeps decisions consistent and explainable. The current implementation is a local hackathon MVP with simulated metrics, grievances, SLA events, and escalation actions; it does not connect to CPGRAMS or perform real government escalation. Its value is a stronger operating model for accountable public service, ready for production integrations.
